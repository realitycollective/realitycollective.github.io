The Reality Collective is an open community run by [Dino Fejzagić](https://github.com/FejZa) and [Simon Jackson](https://github.com/SimonDarksideJ). Its aim is to build the best open-source frameworks for cross-platform XR development, in Unity and on the web.

The Collective maintains three families of projects:

- **Reality Toolkit for Unity**: an extensible toolkit for XR solutions that run on any XR or OpenXR platform Unity supports, with a wide range of tools, extensions and components to speed up Unity development.
- **Service Framework**: a tested service and dependency-injection framework for Unity and TypeScript on the web, so an application's services are declared once and run on every platform.
- **Reality Toolkit WebXR**: engine-free input, interactions, spatial UI and environment for the immersive web, with one adapter per engine for three.js, Babylon.js, Meta IWSDK and Google XR Blocks.

Every project is MIT licensed and developed in the open on [GitHub](https://github.com/realitycollective). Questions, ideas and help are on the [Reality Collective Discord](https://discord.gg/YjHAQD2XT8).
